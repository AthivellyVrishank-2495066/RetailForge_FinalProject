RetailForge - Usage Guide

1. Compile the Project

$ cd /workspaces/RetailForge
$ make clean && make

2. Run the Application

$ ./retailforge

3. Main Menu Options

1. Product Management
2. Supplier Management
3. Sales & Purchases
4. Reports & Analytics
5. Forecasting & Alerts
6. System Backup/Restore
0. Exit

4. Product Management Workflows

- Add New Product
  • Choose perishable or non-perishable
  • Enter ID, name, category, supplier ID, stock, reorder level, cost, price
  • Enter expiry and shelf life for perishable products
  • Enter batch number and warranty for non-perishable products

- View All Products
  • Displays all inventory items with summary details

- Search Product by ID or Name
  • Find an item and view its full details

- Update Product
  • Adjust stock, reorder threshold, or pricing

- Delete Product
  • Remove product from inventory and CSV storage

- Filter Products
  • Filter by category or stock range

5. Supplier Management Workflows

- Add New Supplier
  • Enter supplier ID, name, contact, lead time, reliability score

- View All Suppliers
  • Displays supplier records

- Search Supplier by ID
  • Locate supplier details quickly

- Update Supplier
  • Modify contact, lead time, or reliability data

- Delete Supplier
  • Remove supplier record from inventory

6. Sales & Purchase Workflows

- Record a Sale
  • Enter product ID and quantity
  • System checks current stock and updates inventory
  • Transaction is recorded to `transactions.csv` and `sales.log`

- Record a Purchase
  • Add stock for an existing product
  • Transaction records purchase details

- View Sales History
  • Displays recent sales and purchase transactions

7. Forecasting & Alerts

- View Pending Alerts
  • Lists low stock and near-expiry alerts

- Calculate Moving Average
  • Enter product ID and time window for demand smoothing

- Predict Demand
  • Enter product ID and days ahead for prediction

- Suggest Reorder Quantity
  • Calculates reorder amount based on demand and stock levels

- Generate All Alerts
  • Produces a queue of pending inventory alerts

8. Reports & Analytics

- Low Stock Items
- Near Expiry Items
- Top 20 Best-Selling Products
- Slow-Moving Products
- Category-wise Profit & Loss
- Supplier Performance Report
- System Dashboard

9. Exit

- Choose option 0 from the main menu.
- The system saves data before exit.
